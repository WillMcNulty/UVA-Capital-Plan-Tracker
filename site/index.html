UVA Major Capital Plans · 2021-2026

How UVA pays for its buildings, and how the budgets move.

An independent look at six years of the University of Virginia's public capital plans: the funding mix, 94 projects followed through their renames, and a feasibility model you can push around. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the University.

The linking
94 projects followed across renames by exact, fuzzy and hand-checked matching.
The checks
Every push reruns the tests, and the browser model must match the Python model before the site deploys.
